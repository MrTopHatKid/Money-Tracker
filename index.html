Settings & backups

Remembered tokens are stored in this browser. Scripts on this website can access them. Only remember a restricted, short-lived token on your own device.

Manage entries

Open “Edit entry” to change its amount, type, or description. Delete removes it from your active budget. Both actions create a backup first and recalculate later balances. Weekly allowances cannot be changed here.

Deleted-entry audit

Deleted transactions remain here for reference but do not count toward your budget.
